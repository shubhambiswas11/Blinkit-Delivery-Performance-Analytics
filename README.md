# 🚚 Blinkit Delivery Performance Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes delivery operations for Blinkit using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.  
The goal is to evaluate **ETA accuracy, delivery performance, cancellations, customer ratings, and operational efficiency** across multiple cities.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Measure **delivery KPIs** (average time, cancellations, ratings, order value).
- Identify **bottlenecks** (peak hours, weather impact, partner performance).
- Visualize **customer behavior** (payment methods, ratings distribution).
- Provide **business recommendations** for improving service quality.

## 📊 Key Analyses
- **Orders by City** – demand distribution across regions.  
- **Cancellation Reasons** – breakdown of why orders fail.  
- **Payment Methods** – customer preferences in transactions.  
- **Restaurant Categories** – order distribution by category.  
- **Delivery Partner Performance** – efficiency and ratings comparison.  
- **Peak Hour Analysis** – demand vs delays during busy times.  
- **Weather Impact** – effect of conditions on delivery times.  
- **Customer Ratings** – satisfaction trends and distribution.  
- **Correlation Heatmap** – relationships between distance, time, value, and ratings.

## 📈 Insights
- Cancellation rate is **~48%**, showing operational inefficiencies.  
- Average delivery time (**~49 min**) exceeds ETA (**~36 min**), indicating delays.  
- Peak hours and rainy weather significantly increase delivery times.  
- Partner performance varies widely, impacting customer satisfaction.  
- Ratings cluster around **3/5**, suggesting moderate satisfaction.

## ✅ Recommendations
- Improve **partner training & incentives** to reduce delays.  
- Adjust **staffing during peak hours** to balance demand.  
- Introduce **weather contingency planning** (backup riders, surge pricing).  
- Enhance **customer loyalty programs** to boost ratings.  
- Build a **real-time KPI dashboard** for proactive monitoring.
